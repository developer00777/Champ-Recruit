What happens after you apply

A hiring process you can see

Every application shows its live stage in My applications. No more wondering if anyone read your CV.

  1. AppliedInstant confirmation with your application ID.
  2. ScreeningA recruiter reviews your profile within 3 working days.
  3. AssessmentRole-specific task: a mock cold call, a data sheet or a case.
  4. InterviewHiring-manager round, then a final round for senior roles.
  5. OfferOffer of appointment with full compensation annexure.
How we use AI in hiring

Tools may help sort applications and suggest role matches, like the CV match above. A recruiter reviews every application and makes every shortlisting and hiring decision. You can ask for a human-only review at any stage.

Pro tips by role family
  • Sales & Lead Gen: bring numbers: target, achievement %, meetings booked.
  • HR, TA & L&D: describe one process you improved and what changed.
  • IT & Data: describe one problem you solved end to end, with the result.
Frequently asked
Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes. Save the roles you like and apply to all of them with one profile. Each role is reviewed separately.

How long until I hear back?

A recruiter reviews new applications within 3 working days. Your stage updates in My applications.

Will you email me about other jobs?

Only if you opt in or create a job alert. Applying alone never signs you up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

How long do you keep my data?

As long as needed to process your application, and for a fixed retention period afterwards if you opt in to future roles. You can ask us to delete your profile at any time.

Someone asked me to pay for a job. Is it genuine?

No. We never charge candidates. Do not pay, and report it to the recruitment team.

Accenture review: what we kept, what we fixed

Based on the India job-search page as captured on 24 Oct 2026, with no listings loaded at the time. Re-check on the live site before presenting.

Kept and improved
  • Three-tab structureFind roles, My applications and alerts live on one site, with no hand-off to a separate application portal.
  • Exact-phrase searchQuotes for exact match, plus short forms such as BD, KAM, CRO and TA.
  • Best match and Newest sortingRelevance weights title first, then skills, then description.
  • AI transparencyA plain-language note that a recruiter makes every decision.
  • Skip linksKeyboard users can jump straight to results.
Gaps we closed
  • Filter counter read "Filters -1"Correct active-filter count and removable filter chips.
  • Country selector showed India and United States at onceOne location field per listing; no conflicting global switch.
  • No job alerts on the pageAlert from any search, with its own consent and a stop button.
  • No CV matchingPaste a CV and see matching roles with the skills recognised.
  • Empty state only offered "Try a new search"Empty results offer an alert and one-tap clear.
  • No fraud warning on the search pageRecruitment fraud banner above every search.
  • Application status sits in a separate portalStage tracker and withdraw option on the same site.

What this demo adds for a sales-driven group

Apply-once shortlist

Candidates save several roles and submit one application covering all of them. HR sees one person, many interests.

Arena-match quiz

Three questions route undecided candidates to the right department, reducing wrong-fit applications.

60-second pitch upload

Optional voice or video pitch for sales roles, screened before a call is booked.

Bulk-hiring flag

Roles with 5+ seats (Lead Generation, Data Research) are badged and sortable, for campaign-style drives.

Two separate consents

Processing consent for the application, and an optional, unticked opt-in for future campaigns with unsubscribe on every email.

Bench re-engagement

Recruiters target opted-in past applicants by department, including good-fit "Not selected" candidates, before paying to source again.

Build in three phases

Phase 1 · MVPPortal + candidate database
  • Job listings with search and filters
  • Standard application form, multi-role apply
  • Candidate profile and status tracker
  • Consent capture and unsubscribe
  • Recruiter table with status updates
  • Basic dashboard: volume, funnel, source
Phase 2 · AutomateCampaigns and workflow
  • Re-engagement campaigns by department
  • Automated status emails and SMS / WhatsApp updates
  • Assessment links per role
  • Employee referral tracking
  • Interview scheduling
Phase 3 · IntegrateATS and intelligence
  • ATS / HRMS integration, offer letter hand-off
  • CV parsing and profile prefill
  • Role-to-candidate matching scores
  • Hiring-cost and time-to-hire reporting
  • Data retention and deletion automation
