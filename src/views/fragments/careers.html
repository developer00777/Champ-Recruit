What happens after you apply

A hiring process you can see

Every application shows its live stage in My applications. No more wondering if anyone read your CV.

  1. AppliedInstant confirmation with your application ID.
  2. ScreeningA recruiter reviews your profile within 3 working days.
  3. AssessmentRole-specific task: a mock cold call, a data sheet or a case.
  4. InterviewHiring-manager round, then a final round for senior roles.
  5. OfferOffer of appointment with full compensation annexure.
How we use AI in hiring

Tools may help sort applications and suggest role matches, like the CV match above. A recruiter reviews every application and makes every shortlisting and hiring decision. You can ask for a human-only review at any stage.

Pro tips by role family
  • Sales & Lead Gen: bring numbers: target, achievement %, meetings booked.
  • HR, TA & L&D: describe one process you improved and what changed.
  • IT & Data: describe one problem you solved end to end, with the result.
Frequently asked
Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes. Save the roles you like and apply to all of them with one profile. Each role is reviewed separately.

How long until I hear back?

A recruiter reviews new applications within 3 working days. Your stage updates in My applications.

Will you email me about other jobs?

Only if you opt in or create a job alert. Applying alone never signs you up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

How long do you keep my data?

As long as needed to process your application, and for a fixed retention period afterwards if you opt in to future roles. You can ask us to delete your profile at any time.

Someone asked me to pay for a job. Is it genuine?

No. We never charge candidates. Do not pay, and report it to the recruitment team.
